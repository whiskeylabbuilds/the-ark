THE ARK

ABOUT

An offline library and private AI for the household

The Ark keeps working when the internet doesn't. One small computer holds encyclopedias, medical and repair guides, courses, books, maps, music, notes and a private AI. Every phone, tablet and laptop on the home Wi-Fi can use it, and nothing ever leaves the house.

It's built entirely from free, open-source software and freely licensed content, and anyone can install it on their own hardware.
