The map is cut from a worldwide map for the area you choose, so it needs your region first.

  1. Find your area at https://bboxfinder.com: draw a box and copy the numbers next to "Box".
  2. Add two lines to ~/ark/.env, for example:
    MAP_BBOX=-106.7,25.8,-88.8,37.1
MAP_REGION_NAME=Texas and neighbors
  3. Run:
    cd ~/ark && scripts/build-maps.sh

A state is usually 0.5–2 GB. This page turns into the map when it finishes.
